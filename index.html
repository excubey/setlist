    Every spin class is a playlist you never wrote down.

    SpinTracker listens while you ride, names the songs playing around you, and ties each one to the effort you actually put in.

    Then watch the ride come back, as a film you can post.

    Track the ride

    Pair any standard Bluetooth heart rate strap, cadence sensor or power meter. Your strap reconnects on its own when you start a ride, and last time's bike is one tap away. No sensors? The ride still records its whole music history and duration.

    Find your power songs

    Every track gets its own effort profile — average and peak heart rate, cadence, and where it landed in the class. Over enough rides your library surfaces the songs you consistently ride hardest to.

    Keep the music

    Turn any ride's tracklist into an Apple Music playlist in one tap. Take the whole class or just the songs that earned it.

    New in 1.2. Mostly for the people at the front of the room.

    • If you teach, your classes are yours

      Turn on the instructor profile and the classes you teach are kept apart from the rides you take, in History, your song library and your recaps. Name a class once and SpinTracker remembers it for that studio.

    • Check the set you taught

      Attach the Apple Music playlist you taught to, then check what actually played against it and fix the tracklist.

    • Credit everyone who taught

      A co-taught class can carry up to four instructors, and your booking link shows as a Book button on every class you share.

    • The Anime Replay

      Your ride as a short film: a road that climbs on the songs you worked hardest to. In 3D on iOS 26, or as a 2D side-scroll.

    • Templates and tags

      Start a card from a template. Tag the studio, instructor and class from the share screen, and History shows them on every ride.

    • Two new card styles

      Cyberpunk is neon on a night ground. Shonen is a manga page in black ink with a red accent.

    • Rides save to Apple Health

      Once Health is connected, every ride is saved on its own.

    • Short setlist links

      A shared setlist is a short link now, good for 30 days.
